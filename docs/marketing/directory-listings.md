Each listing is a sign-up or form submission, so **nothing is submitted until Akeem says go**. Use the copy below
word for word everywhere, so every directory says the same thing. No "AI", no phone or POS, no posting-for-you claims,
no invented numbers.

**Short description (≤160 chars)**
KOB drafts your Google review replies and listing fixes every morning. You approve, tap once, and post. Nothing goes out without your OK.

**Long description**
KOB is the restaurant manager for independent restaurants and cafés with one to five locations. Every morning it
checks your Google listing, reviews and website, then sends a short brief of what needs you today. It reads your
reviews and drafts the replies in your voice, holding angry or sensitive ones for you. It drafts listing fixes for
hours, holiday hours, your description and posts. You approve, tap once, and post. KOB also gives you a review link and
QR table cards to get more reviews from happy guests. Keep your website and booking system. 7 days free, no card, then
$99/month per location (£79 in the UK).

**Features list (where a site asks)**
Review reply drafts · Google listing checks · Holiday hours reminders · Morning brief · Review link and QR table cards · Website checks · Approve before anything goes out
