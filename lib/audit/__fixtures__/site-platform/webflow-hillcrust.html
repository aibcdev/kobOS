About Us

The Crust!

HillCrust Pizza brings a modern take on timeless Neapolitan style pizza to San Diego, built from the ground up on flavor, craft, and patience. Our dough is made with 00 non GMO unbleached flour and fermented over five days to develop aroma, texture, and depth because great pizza starts long before it reaches the oven. Every pie reflects our commitment to high quality ingredients and a menu designed to satisfy both classic cravings and creative tastes.

Beyond pizza, HillCrust is known for garlic bread, cheesecake, Caesar salad, meatballs, and vegetarian favorites, including thoughtfully crafted vegan options made with high quality vegan cheese and plant based meats. We also offer gluten free crust options so everyone can enjoy a great slice. From turkey gourmet pies to customizable Margheritas, we give you the freedom to shape your perfect pizza while keeping flavor at the center of everything we do.

Stop by, take out, or order delivery and discover why HillCrust Pizza is quickly becoming a neighborhood favorite.

Our Cuisine

Dough First!

HillCrust Pizza centers its menu around modern Neapolitan style pizza, paired with a lineup of Italian inspired favorites made with high quality ingredients. From Margherita and cheese pizzas to garlic bread, meatballs, Caesar salad, and cheesecake, our offerings balance classic comfort with thoughtful craft. We also proudly offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten free options, including hearty sandwiches and customizable pies designed to deliver full flavor without compromise.
